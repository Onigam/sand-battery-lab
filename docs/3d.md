# The 3D worlds

Four worlds built from `src/three/dims.ts` (metres, the one source, every constant tagged `reported`, `derived` or `estimate`; what the simulation owns is imported from `PORNAINEN`). `src/three/index.ts` exports `createSiteScene`, `createSiloScene`, `createPipeCellScene`, `createPlantRoomScene`, all `lazyModule`s, and `WORLD` (the `SceneDef` fields of each). Ids as frozen in `docs/research.md` §4: `site` 13, `silo` 14, `pipe-cell` 9, `plant-room` 15, 51 in all. No text in any texture, no canvas, no file.

## Frames

**Silo frame**: Y up from the ground at the foot of the slab, the axis at x = z = 0, azimuth from +X towards +Z. The `silo` and `plant-room` worlds are drawn in it directly; `site` moves the same pieces by `SITE.silo` (`siteFromSilo`). The stack: slab 0 to 0.5, base insulation to 1, stone to 7, top insulation to 8, deck to 8.12, cladding to the eaves at 11.5, roof to 13 (the split is an estimate that sums to the reported 13 m).

## One machine, one function

| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `machine/silo.ts`      | slab, base and wall insulation, shell, cladding, top insulation, deck, roof, the two high openings, ladder, lances; its section polygons; the water pipes and cable riser on the wall | all three; `site` with `{ inside: false }`                                                                                |
| `machine/equipment.ts` | the fifteen machines of the room and their flow paths; the long runs of four of them (`services`)                                                                                     | `plant-room` as parts, `silo` merged into `equipment-floor`                                                               |
| `machine/pipes.ts`     | leg, bend, loop, header, spacer, the lattice                                                                                                                                          | `silo` instanced (566 legs; the model's 616 is the bed's area over the pitch squared), `pipe-cell` at a higher resolution |
| `machine/room.ts`      | rails, lamps, walkway, guard rail, workbench, shelves, trolley, extinguisher                                                                                                          | `silo`, `plant-room`                                                                                                      |
| `scale.ts`             | the scale of the false colour: a bar on a stand, ticks each 100 °C, the same ramp as the stone                                                                                        | `silo` on the paper, `pipe-cell` on the paper, `plant-room` on the deck, leaning back                                     |

`machine.test.ts` holds them within 3 mm across the worlds. Each return bend rests on a small block standing on the floor of the bed (`bendBlockGeometry`, height `PIPE.bendClearance`): the assembly guide sets the U on it, the stone then buries it, and a test holds the contact.

**In `silo` nothing stands outside a part** but the paper and its contact shadow: the water pipes and the cable riser are parented to `outer-cladding`, so that they hide and rise with the wall they are fixed to, and the scale to `storage-bed`, whose colours it explains. It stays on the paper when the stone rises in the exploded view (the part's `custom` motion), and it is not cut. `machine.test.ts` measures the cladding and the stone without them.

**The long runs of a machine are not in its box.** A heat meter is a meter on a pipe, not 5.5 m of pipe. The pipes of `dh-pump` and `heat-meter`, the trays and cables of `blower-drive`, the conduit of `control-cabinet` are built apart (`EquipmentPieces.services`): no part of the box, of the outline, or of the explode; each run follows the display of its machine in `plantroom/scene.ts`, so that it shows while its machine does. They are not picked: a click goes through them.

## The camera

**The depth range.** `Stage.setActive` gives every world but `site` a near plane of 0.01 and a far plane of 60. At 24 bits a depth step is then 1 cm at 40 m, the valleys of the ribs stand 5 mm off the wool, and the wall flashed yellow from behind. `holdDepthRange` (`world.ts`) sets 0.1 and 150 on the camera in `scene.onBeforeRender`: the renderer reads the projection after that hook, and each world change goes through it again. A step is now 2 mm at 60 m, and the closest any world lets the camera come is 0.5 m. Fog starts at 100 m in `silo` and 95 m in `plant-room`, behind everything the camera can frame; the paper dissolves by itself. ## The physics drives the picture

| Reads                                                                                | Does                                                                                                                                    |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `bedT[ring + rings*layer]`                                                           | the bed's false colour, a half-float texture of the model's grid (10 × 6), written at 8 Hz, read with a linear filter; lines every 50 K |
| `onDrawnSilo`                                                                        | off the drawn silo the section goes to plain stone (the medium's colour)                                                                |
| `zones[].mode`, dampers                                                              | blades and pointers (travel of about 1 s drawn), flow dots by zone                                                                      |
| `chargeBlowerAngle`, `dischargeBlowerAngle`, `pumpAngle`                             | rotors: wheel seen through its port, shaft, coupling with a mark. The model caps them at 2 turns a second, so no blur disc is needed    |
| `heaterGlow`, `heater`                                                               | the elements' false-colour glow, lamps of the controller                                                                                |
| `chargeBlower`, `dischargeBlower`                                                    | lamps of the drive, speed of the dots                                                                                                   |
| `airHot`, `chargeReturnT`, `exchangerAirIn`, `exchangerAirOut`, `supplyT`, `returnT` | the colour of each flow's dots, on the same ramp as the stone                                                                           |
| `getPipeProfile(ring, layer)` (passed as an option, not in the state)                | the rings of heat around each leg in `pipe-cell`, at 5 Hz, lines every 20 K                                                             |
| `sim.params.medium` (passed as an option)                                            | the look of the stone, soapstone or sand                                                                                                |
| `winter`, `day`, `hour`, `boiler`, `price`, `heater`, `waterFlow`                    | the village: `docs/scenery.md`                                                                                                          |

**Not used:** `elementT` (the glow, derived from it by the model, is enough), `maxT`, `minT`, `meanT`, `coreT`, `edgeT`, `shellT`, `surfaceT` (they are in the strip and the panel), `chargeDp`, `dischargeDp`, `tripped`, `alarms`.

**The sand switch.** Under the false colour soapstone and sand look alike, and the temperatures do not move when the medium changes, so the switch showed nothing at the default views. `StoneReveal` (`heat.ts`) answers it: when the medium changes, the section shows the new stone in its own colour for 1.6 s, fades back in 1.4 s, in `silo`, `pipe-cell` and `plant-room`. Measured at 1280 px with the clock stopped: 7.4 %, 17.4 % and 4.2 % of the view change, none of it 4 s later. The dampers' blades follow `zones[].chargeDamper` and `dischargeDamper` live: a blade goes from open to closed in about 2 s, read off the rotation in the browser.

**The conflict of the research:** research §0.4 draws the charge blower after the resistors, where it would see 500 °C. The model has it on the return side, upstream of them (physics §1.4). **The 3D follows the model**: return duct → blower → resistors → hot header; `machine.test.ts` holds it.

**Claims:** the equipment floor is a reading, not a published fact. Nothing on screen says otherwise: labels name the parts, and the overview text belongs to the data role.

## Cut

`cut.ts`: one tool, two cuts, two clipping planes with `clipIntersection`, so pressing the tool compiles nothing. On arrival a quarter is removed (x > 0, z > 0), and the section shows on two faces; the tool removes the whole front half (z > 0). Every machine of the room stands in z < 0, so neither cut goes through one. Caps are polygons in (r, y) on three quarter-planes, in unclipped copies of the part's material; legs, bends, lances and ducts lying on a plane have their own strips over the coloured stone. A vertical explode keeps a section valid: the planes contain Y.

`plant-room` has no tool: it keeps the quarter open below the deck, and above the deck the wall opens towards the camera (`dollhouse`, a discard in the shader). The rails and lamps under the ceiling have the same opening, tested on the two ends of their line (`overheadDoll`): a rail is drawn while the wall stands at both its ends, so it is whole or it is not there, and it never stops in mid-air. From the home side all five are there, in a paler grey, with no shadow across the deck. From the side or from behind they go, and the rods of the ducts end at the height of a ceiling that is not drawn either.

## False colour

`heat.ts`: one ramp, fixed from 0 to 600 °C, blue to amber to crimson. `heat.test.ts` runs it through both tone curves of the kit (ACES at 1 and 1.18, Neutral at 1.05, 1.1 and 1.2) and checks that the steps stay apart, none clipped or black. A part of the colour is emissive, so a shadow never hides a temperature. The three coloured worlds have the same scale (`scale.ts`) with the kit's labels: 500, 300 and 100 °C in `silo`, one label « 0 à 600 °C » in `pipe-cell` and `plant-room`, whose main labels are full (`expansion-loop` went to the minor labels for it). In `plant-room` no part is the stone, so a click on that label goes to `hot-air-duct`. Stone at 400 °C does not glow: that sentence is in the overview, once.

In `plant-room` the dots of the charge are the return's colour through the blower up to the resistors, and hot from there on.

## Labels

« All labels » at 1280, the 3D view of 842 × 678: 0 over another in `silo`, `pipe-cell` and `plant-room`, 1 or 2 in `site`, in both languages (`review.test.ts`). The anchors were found by a search over the surface of each part, against all four sizes, and hard-coded. The layers of the silo stay on their section on +X, where they are framed when selected. On a phone and at 768 the lab can only do so much: 6 to 13 in `silo`, 13 to 15 in `plant-room`, 7 or 8 in `site`, 1 or 2 in `pipe-cell`, and up to 7 minor labels of a world run past the edge of the view; 16 labels do not fit on 390 px without the kit's declutter. « Principales » is clean at all sizes (`labels.test.ts`). `crushed-soapstone`'s label stands on the plinth of the block beside its tray: on a phone the tray is at the edge of the view, and its name has to fit.

**The X-ray of the room.** With nothing selected `xrayShell` clears the ducts and the dampers, so that the machines read. With a part selected, a ghost draws only the largest mesh of each part (`lightGhosts`): fifteen machines over one floor, at the kit's opacity, added up to dark blotches.

## For the lead

`WORLD[id]` spreads into each `SceneDef`. `partLinks`: `silo-exterior` → `silo`, `equipment-floor` → `plant-room`, `pipe-field` → `pipe-cell`.

`slowMo` is off in `silo` and `pipe-cell`, where nothing turns; it scales only the visual rotations, and without it the toolbar keeps to one row at 1280 px.

**`site` has no explode offsets**: the school hung 9 m up, and it taught nothing. The tool stays on the toolbar and does nothing until the kit can hide it (`docs/kit-requests.md`). So the steps of the installation guide, which play there, no longer drop their parts in from above: they appear, framed and outlined.
