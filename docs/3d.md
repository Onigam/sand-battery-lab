# The 3D worlds

Four worlds built from `src/three/dims.ts` (metres, the one source, every constant tagged `reported`, `derived` or `estimate`; what the simulation owns is imported from `PORNAINEN`). `src/three/index.ts` exports `createSiteScene`, `createSiloScene`, `createPipeCellScene`, `createPlantRoomScene`, all `lazyModule`s, and `WORLD` (the `SceneDef` fields of each). Ids as frozen in `docs/research.md` §4: `site` 13, `silo` 14, `pipe-cell` 9, `plant-room` 15, 51 in all. No text in any texture, no canvas, no file.

## Frames

**Silo frame**: Y up from the ground at the foot of the slab, the axis at x = z = 0, azimuth from +X towards +Z. The `silo` and `plant-room` worlds are drawn in it directly; `site` moves the same pieces by `SITE.silo` (`siteFromSilo`). The stack: slab 0 to 0.5, base insulation to 1, stone to 7, top insulation to 8, deck to 8.12, cladding to the eaves at 11.5, roof to 13 (the split is an estimate that sums to the reported 13 m).

## The physics drives the picture

| Reads                                                                                | Does                                                                                                                                    |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `bedT[ring + rings*layer]`                                                           | the bed's false colour, a half-float texture of the model's grid (10 × 6), written at 8 Hz, read with a linear filter; lines every 50 K |
| `onDrawnSilo`                                                                        | off the drawn silo the section goes to plain stone (the medium's colour)                                                                |
| `zones[].mode`, dampers                                                              | blades and pointers (travel of about 1 s drawn), flow dots by zone                                                                      |
| `chargeBlowerAngle`, `dischargeBlowerAngle`, `pumpAngle`                             | rotors: wheel seen through its port, shaft, coupling with a mark. The model caps them at 2 turns a second, so no blur disc is needed    |
| `heaterGlow`, `heater`                                                               | the elements' false-colour glow, lamps of the controller                                                                                |
| `chargeBlower`, `dischargeBlower`                                                    | lamps of the drive, speed of the dots                                                                                                   |
| `airHot`, `chargeReturnT`, `exchangerAirIn`, `exchangerAirOut`, `supplyT`, `returnT` | the colour of each flow's dots, on the same ramp as the stone                                                                           |
| `getPipeProfile(ring, layer)` (passed as an option, not in the state)                | the rings of heat around each leg in `pipe-cell`, at 5 Hz, lines every 20 K                                                             |
| `sim.params.medium` (passed as an option)                                            | the look of the stone, soapstone or sand                                                                                                |
| `winter`, `day`, `hour`, `boiler`, `price`, `heater`, `waterFlow`                    | the village: `docs/scenery.md`                                                                                                          |

**Not used:** `elementT` (the glow, derived from it by the model, is enough), `maxT`, `minT`, `meanT`, `coreT`, `edgeT`, `shellT`, `surfaceT` (they are in the strip and the panel), `chargeDp`, `dischargeDp`, `tripped`, `alarms`, `angle`. `DemoState` and `state.angle` are in `src/sim/engine.ts`, which this role does not edit: nothing here reads them.

**The conflict of the research:** research §0.4 draws the charge blower after the resistors, where it would see 500 °C. The model has it on the return side, upstream of them (physics §1.4). **The 3D follows the model**: return duct → blower → resistors → hot header; `machine.test.ts` holds it.

**Claims:** the equipment floor is a reading, not a published fact. Nothing on screen says otherwise: labels name the parts, and the overview text belongs to the data role.

## Cut

`cut.ts`: one tool, two cuts, two clipping planes with `clipIntersection`, so pressing the tool compiles nothing. On arrival a quarter is removed (x > 0, z > 0), and the section shows on two faces; the tool removes the whole front half (z > 0). Every machine of the room stands in z < 0, so neither cut goes through one. Caps are polygons in (r, y) on three quarter-planes, in unclipped copies of the part's material; legs, bends, lances and ducts lying on a plane have their own strips over the coloured stone. A vertical explode keeps a section valid: the planes contain Y.

`plant-room` has no tool: it keeps the quarter open below the deck, and above the deck the wall opens towards the camera (`dollhouse`, a discard in the shader).

## False colour

`heat.ts`: one ramp, fixed from 0 to 600 °C, blue to amber to crimson. `heat.test.ts` runs it through both tone curves of the kit (ACES at 1 and 1.18, Neutral at 1.05, 1.1 and 1.2) and checks that the steps stay apart, none clipped or black. A part of the colour is emissive, so a shadow never hides a temperature. The scale in the `silo` world is a bar on a stand with the kit's labels. Stone at 400 °C does not glow: that sentence is in the overview, once.

## Budgets

Colour pass, measured by `drawStats` in `worlds.test.ts` (limits there). The shadow pass adds about as many calls again.

| World        | Draw calls | Triangles | First build (Node on this Mac, shared maps included in the first) |
| ------------ | ---------- | --------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `site`       | 124        | 348,000   | 566 ms                                                            |
| `silo`       | 121        | 198,000   | 287 ms                                                            |
| `pipe-cell`  | 24         | 119,000   | 56 ms                                                             |
| `plant-room` | 147        | 81,000    | 92 ms                                                             |

Frame rate on a real phone: **not measured**. `FrameGovernor` of the kit lowers the pixel ratio by itself on a slow device.

Shimmer (`tools/review/flicker.mjs`, 1000 × 640, 90 frames, Metal), slow orbit / slow dolly, with the largest frame jump:

| World        | Orbit  | Dolly  | Spike |
| ------------ | ------ | ------ | ----- |
| `site`       | 7.97 % | 9.70 % | 1.31  |
| `silo`       | 1.08 % | 4.29 % | 1.41  |
| `pipe-cell`  | 0.32 % | 1.77 % | 1.99  |
| `plant-room` | 2.01 % | 5.88 % | 1.38  |

Mill Lab's site gave 2.6 % in an orbit at 1280 px, so **`site` is above its neighbours**: dark spruces on white snow, the highest contrast a forest can have. Past 130 m a spruce already blends to one colour and one lit surface, and the far forest floor is dark; more would wash the near trees out. A map of where it shimmers shows the far forest band first, then edges in motion. No pop: every spike is under 2.
