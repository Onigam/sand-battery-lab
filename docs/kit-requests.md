# Kit requests (what `@onigam/lab-kit` 0.5.1 lacks, from the physics and panel work of Sand Battery Lab)

Numbered, one paragraph each. Nothing here was worked around by patching the kit; each item says what the lab did instead.

1. **A widget to paint hours.** The lab's game (lesson 5, research Q8): a visitor paints the hours of a week in which the silo charges, over a curve of prices. The kit has `slider`, `select`, `toggle`, `section` only. Asked: a `timeline` widget, a row or a grid of N cells that can be dragged across, with an optional curve behind it, `timeline(label, values: boolean[], opts: { cellsPerRow, rowLabels, background?: number[] }, on)`. Until then the panel paints blocks of three hours with `part-chip` buttons, seven rows of eight, which reads but takes a screen on a phone. The engine already takes a plan of 168 booleans, so only that part of the panel changes. The gravity lab may want the same widget.
2. **`sim/thermal` to move into the kit, when its second user arrives.** `src/sim/thermal/` is pure, in SI with °C, with no import from the lab or the kit and no lab-specific name, and a test holds both. The next users are the mass heater and the chauffe-eau (week of 9 November). Move as it is; its interface is in `docs/physics.md` §2. Material tables for brick, concrete and wood come with the lab that sources them.
3. **`sim/market`, on the lead's decision.** `src/sim/market/` (dynamic programming over a store with an hourly price, a charging limit, a loss and a backup) is equally self-contained. The gravity battery's question may want it this week; if so, the kit is the place, and whichever lab goes second moves it.
4. **Speed labels for a lab that runs on a calendar.** The strip writes `×3600`, `×86400`, `×604800`, which says nothing to a visitor and is wide on a phone (Mill Lab has the same). Asked: `speeds` entries with a label, `{ value: 86400, label: l('1 j/s', '1 d/s') }`. The lab passes four numbers for now.
5. **A long clock.** Every lab that follows the seasons writes the same `day of year → 15 Feb` in its strip (`monthOf` in `src/panels/strip.ts` here; Mill Lab's `site.ts` has its own). Request 8 of Mill Lab; still open.
6. **Tags on a figure.** The lab rule is that every number shown carries its tag (`†` a model result, `*` an estimate). The `.readout` box has no place for one: the panel appends it to the unit in `<small>`. A `readout({ label, value, unit, tag })` widget (Mill Lab's request 6) with a legend once per panel would make the rule cheap to follow. The same for the kit's `LineChart` axes: a chart's unit has to live in the title today, because a `yFmt` with a unit is cut at the left edge above three characters (`00 MWh` for `100 MWh`, seen at 768 px). A `yUnit` option, or a gutter measured from the labels, would remove that trap.
7. **`section` that starts closed, `num`, `months`, the fake DOM.** Mill Lab's requests 2, 3 and 5 are still open. `src/panels/test-dom.ts` here is again a copy, `num` a fourth version.
8. **A section coloured from a field: a false-colour legend.** For the 3D role: `silo` world colours the bed from `state.bedT`, and a legend in °C that says once it is false colour (stone at 400 °C does not glow) is the first of its kind. A kit `heatLegend(min, max, label)` overlay and a shared colour ramp would keep the thermal labs alike (the poêle and the bioclimatic house want the same). No workaround yet: asked before wave 2 starts, the 3D role decides.
9. **Part categories that a lab can name.** `PartCategory` is a closed list of five (`hydraulic`, `mechanical`, `electrical`, `structure`, `site`), with labels in the kit. A heat store has no use for « Hydraulique » on an air loop, and the research's own categories (storage, heat transfer, air, network, control) have nowhere to go. Asked: `catLabels` in `LabDefinition`, or a sixth `thermal` category; the poêle, the chauffe-eau and the bioclimatic house will want it too. Until then `src/data/ids.ts` maps them: water is `hydraulic`, the air loop and its pipes `mechanical`, the stone and what holds it `structure`.
10. **Bilingual `duration` and `torque` on a guide step.** `GuideStep.duration` is a plain string shown as it is, so a reported total such as « about a year » has no way to read in both languages. The lab leaves `duration` empty on every step and writes the reported totals in the text. Asked: `duration?: L | string`.
11. **A bilingual scale in the title block.** `TitleBlock.scale` is typed `string`, though a diagram's scale is a word (« schéma », « diagram »). The sheets resolve it with `tr()` at render time, which is fine as long as a sheet is re-rendered at each language change. Asked: `scale: Text`, like the title.

## From the 3D role (wave 2)

9. **A false-colour legend, answer to request 8.** The `silo` world draws its scale as a bar on a stand, on the paper beside the silo, with three of the kit's hotspot labels for the temperatures (500, 300, 100 °C): no layout CSS, no text in a texture, and a film captured with no interface still has the colours. The sentence « false colour » is in the world's overview, said once. A kit `heatLegend` would still be welcome for the poêle and the bioclimatic house, with the ramp of `src/three/heat.ts` (fixed in °C, tested through both tone curves).
10. **`Stage.cast` and `clipIntersection`.** Picking skips a hit if any clipping plane has the point on its negative side, as with a union of planes. A wedge (`clipIntersection: true`, two planes, a quarter removed) would refuse three quarters of the machine. `src/three/cut.ts` works around it with a `Plane` subclass whose `distanceToPoint` answers for the two together. Asked: honour `material.clipIntersection` in `cast`.
11. **A far plane per world.** `Stage.setActive` gives 4000 m to the id `site` and 60 m to every other world. A 15 m silo, framed on a phone or exploded to 31 m, needs its camera at up to 54 m, so its distance is clamped and its paper floor has to dissolve before the far plane (`paperFloor(…, fade)`). Asked: `SceneModule.far?: number`.
12. **What three labs now copy.** `merge.ts`, `detail.ts`, `FlowDots`, `paperFloor`, `framing.ts`, the noise of `textures.ts` and the sky rig come from Mill Lab with small changes (`merge.ts` gained a `scope`, so that a world that clips its materials does not clip another's; the sky gained a held hour and a latitude). They belong in the kit.
13. **A camera for `frameParts` that sees its part.** `frameParts` keeps the current direction, so a machine low behind another (a pump behind a blower) can be framed with something between it and the camera. Asked: a `frameParts` option that lifts the camera until the part's centre is not occluded, or a per-part `viewDir`.
14. **`update` without a camera.** A wall that opens towards the camera needs the camera's position: the plant room uses the shader's `cameraPosition` (`dollhouse` in `src/three/plantroom/scene.ts`). The review tool `walk.mjs` also times out under SwiftShader on a world of this size: `GL=metal`, as in `flicker.mjs`, would help.
