# The water of the 3D worlds

There is no open water in this lab: hot water shows only as a flow inside pipes. The dots of « Flux » in `site` and `plant-room` go at a speed that follows `state.waterFlow`, in a colour read from the same false-colour ramp as the stone (`supplyT`, `returnT`), and only while the model moves water. Nothing else is drawn, so there is nothing decorative to list.
